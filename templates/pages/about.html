About GoPhoto

A Photo Management Solution Written In Go

GoPhoto is a photo management application primarily written in Go. It is designed to provide users with a simple and efficient way to upload and manage their photos.

User Accounts

Secure authentication and personalized experience

Album Management

Organize photos into custom albums effortlessly

Cloud Storage

Configure GoPhoto to use local storage or AWS S3

Session Management
